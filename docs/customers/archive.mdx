---
title: "Archive a customer"
description: "Archive a customer you no longer bill in Flexprice, keeping their details, invoices and payment history available for reporting instead of deleting them"
---

As businesses evolve, some customers may become inactive due to churn, contract expiration, or strategic decisions. Instead of permanently deleting customer data, archiving allows you to preserve historical records while preventing further transactions or modifications. This ensures compliance with data retention policies while keeping your active customer list clean and manageable.

In the dashboard, archive a customer from their page with **Archive customer**. The **Customers** list offers the same action as **Delete customer** in each row's **⋯** menu. Either way, Flexprice keeps the customer's record and marks it archived instead of erasing it.

<Note>
  Cancel all of the customer's contracts first. Flexprice refuses to archive a customer while any of their contracts is not cancelled, or while the customer has an active wallet.
</Note>

**Steps to archive a customer**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the customer's menu">
    Open the customer from **Customers**, click the **⋯** menu at the top right of their page, and select **Archive customer**.

    <Frame>
      ![Customer page menu with Archive customer](/images/docs/customers/archive/archive-customer-option.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm">
    In the confirmation dialog, click **Archive customer**, or **Cancel** if you change your mind. The dashboard can't undo this.

    <Frame>
      ![Archive customer confirmation dialog](/images/docs/customers/archive/confirm-archive.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

Please note:

* **No New Transactions**: The customer can no longer be billed or invoiced.

* **Data Retention**: Customer details, invoices, and payment history remain accessible for reporting purposes.
