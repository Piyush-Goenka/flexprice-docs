---
title: "Archiving a plan"
description: "Archive a pricing plan from the Flexprice dashboard so new customers can no longer subscribe to it, while keeping its history instead of deleting it"
---

Over time, businesses may need to modify or discontinue certain pricing plans due to changes in product offerings, evolving customer needs, or strategic shifts. Instead of deleting plans, archiving retires a plan you no longer offer while keeping its historical data.

**Steps to archive a plan**

* Go to **Product catalog** → **Plans**.

* Click the **⋯** menu next to the plan you want to archive and select **Archive plan**. The same action is in the **⋯** menu on the plan's page.

  <Frame>
    ![Plan row menu with Archive plan](/images/docs/product-catalogue/plans/archive/archive-plan-option.png)
  </Frame>

* A confirmation dialog appears. You can no longer modify the plan details once it is archived. Click **Archive plan** to proceed, or **Cancel** if you change your mind.

<Note>
  A plan can be archived only when no contract uses it, apart from cancelled ones. While another contract is on the plan, Flexprice refuses with "Please remove the active subscriptions before deleting this plan." Cancel those contracts first.
</Note>
